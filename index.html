Smart glasses for people who are blind or have low vision

AI-powered glasses that help people navigate, recognise objects and handle everyday tasks with more independence.

Opened up, every component

Camera module, AI processor board, battery, open-ear speakers, microphones, touch controls and a privacy light, packed into the frame and arms. SpectraSense comes in multiple models, including one that does all of its processing on the glasses.

Live: see it work in real time

The camera captures the scene, the processor finds what matters, and the speaker says it, in a fraction of a second. Steps, signs, bus numbers, text and currency. No face recognition, by design.

Hear it in the live demo →

Hear what SpectraSense sees

This is how SpectraSense talks a wearer through a scene: safety first, then signs and text, then the bigger picture, using clock-face directions and distances in steps.

Example scene
SpectraSense says

Zebra crossing at 12 o'clock, about six steps ahead. The pedestrian signal is red, so wait. A bus is stopped at 2 o'clock; its sign reads 271. Two people are waiting on your left, an arm's length away. The kerb drops in about four steps.
